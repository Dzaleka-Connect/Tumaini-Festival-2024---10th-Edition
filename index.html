Our Story

About the festival

Refugees, the Malawian host community, artists, and visitors built a public space for culture inside Dzaleka. This site keeps a record of it.

Founded in 2014 by Congolese poet Menes La Plume, Tumaini (Swahili for “hope”) grew from a camp gathering into one of Malawi's best-known arts events, with music, poetry, theatre, and dance.

It is held each year inside the camp. Entry is free, and camp-based performers share the bill with Malawian and international artists.
